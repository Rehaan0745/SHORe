About SHOЯe

More than a Fest. A Feeling.

SHOЯe is the annual flagship festival of GITAM Deemed to be University, Visakhapatnam: a student-led celebration where culturals, sports, management and technology meet on the shore of the Bay of Bengal.

Our story

Every shore is where a new story begins.

The beautiful, hustling shores of Vizag inspire the spirit of SHOЯe, just as the calm breeze and crashing waves reflect the youthful energy of Gitamites. Being a student-led university fest, the core committee, the SIGs and the clubs of GITAM come together every year to make the fest happen. The 5th Edition, themed New Beginnings, runs on 18, 19 and 20 December 2026.

The campus

GITAM Visakhapatnam

Founded in 1980, GITAM is NAAC A++ accredited and ranked #88 among universities in NIRF 2025, with 28,000+ students from 52 countries and 110 programmes across 14 disciplines. The Visakhapatnam campus is its founding campus and main hub, at Gandhi Nagar, Rushikonda, set between the Eastern Ghats and the Bay of Bengal.

Good to know

Before you arrive.

Based on previous editions. Rules for the 5th Edition will be confirmed on the Passes and Schedule pages.

How do I register?

On the SHOЯe website, or through the QR codes and registration desks placed around the Visakhapatnam campus.

What do I carry to the concert nights?

A digital copy of your ticket, which is mailed to you after registration, and your identity card.

Is transport provided?

No. Transportation is not provided by the SHOЯe committee or the university. See "How to reach" on the Sports page for routes.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds were not offered in previous editions if you change your mind after purchasing a ticket.
